Food and Drinks

Try foods you are interested in and ask questions when ordering. This list is a simple place to begin.

Pepián
A traditional Guatemalan stew that may be served with rice or tortillas.
Tortillas
Fresh corn tortillas are common with many meals.
Guatemalan Coffee
Antigua is known for coffee, so a local coffee shop can be a nice stop during a walk.
Hot Chocolate
Chocolate drinks are another simple option to look for.
